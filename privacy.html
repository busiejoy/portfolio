Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This website belongs to Busayo M. Odedeji, trading as BM Web Studio. This page explains what information you share when you use the site, and what happens to it.

What I collect

Email form. If you use the email form, I receive the name, email address and message you type in.

WhatsApp form. The WhatsApp form doesn’t send anything to me directly. It writes a message from the details you enter and opens WhatsApp, and I only receive it if you choose to send it.

Direct messages. If you message me on WhatsApp or another platform, I see whatever you send, including your phone number or profile details.

How I use it

I use your details only to reply to you, to discuss your project and, if we work together, to deliver it. I don’t sell your information and I don’t send marketing messages you haven’t asked for.

Services that handle data for this site

  • Web3Forms delivers email-form messages to my inbox.
  • WhatsApp carries the messages you send me there, under its own privacy terms.
  • Vercel hosts the website and, like most hosts, keeps basic technical logs such as your IP address and browser type.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typeface, so your browser contacts Google’s servers when the page loads.

These services have their own privacy policies, and I don’t control how they handle data.

Cookies and storage

This site doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies. It saves one small setting in your browser, your light or dark mode choice, so the site remembers it on your next visit. You can clear it from your browser settings at any time.

How long I keep your messages

I keep messages only as long as I need them to reply to you and to work on a project with you.

Your choices

You can ask me what information I have from you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Message me on WhatsApp or through the email form on the website and I’ll deal with your request.

Changes to this page

If I change how the site works, for example by adding visitor analytics, I’ll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Send me a message through the contact section of the website.
