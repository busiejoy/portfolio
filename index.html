Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you message me.

How long does a website take?

Most simple websites take a few days to complete. The timeline depends on the website's features, complexity and how quickly I receive the required content.

What do you need from me?

I'll need your business name, services, prices, photos, location, contact details and any other information you'd like included on your website.

Will it work on phones?

Yes! Every website I build is designed to work across mobile phones, tablets and desktop computers.

Can customers book or contact me from the website?

Yes! I can integrate contact forms, booking forms and WhatsApp links to make it easier for customers to reach you.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the type of website, its features and the complexity of the project. Contact me with your requirements for a personalised quote.

Can I request changes later?

Yes! You can request updates to your website's content, images, prices and other details. Additional charges may apply depending on the changes required.
